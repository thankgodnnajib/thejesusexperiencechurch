03 — ABOUT US
Pastor ThankGod Nnaji
LEAD PASTOR, THE JESUS EXPERIENCE CHURCH INTERNATIONAL

Meet our pastor.

The Jesus Experience Church is led by Pastor ThankGod Nnaji — he is passionate about knowing the Lord and making Him known, and about seeing people turn to Jesus and experience the new life in Christ.

Drawing on years of pastoral experience, he stepped out in faith to begin this work — trusting a clear and personal calling: to make Jesus known through a practical, everyday approach, and to reach those who feel far from God.
